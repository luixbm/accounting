Operating manual

Simple Accounting Handbook

A working guide to the in-house double-entry accounting system — setting it up, running the books day to day, and producing the monthly financial, operational and sales reports.

CodeIgniter 4 · Shield auth · MySQL/MariaDB  |  keep alongside DEPLOY.md & docs/API.md
Concepts

How the app works

Everything is a journal under the hood. Invoices, payments, transfers and manual entries all post balanced debit/credit lines to the general ledger; every report is read back from those posted lines.

The five ideas to hold in your head

Company
The books are multi-company. Pick the active company in the sidebar; everything you see and enter belongs to it. Each company has its own base currency (IDR for the Indonesian entities, MYR / HKD for the others).
Base currency
Every line stores both the transaction amount and a base-currency amount (*_base). Reports always read the base amounts, so a mixed-currency month still foots.
Document lifecycle
draft → posted → void. Drafts touch nothing. Posting writes the journal. Voiding does not delete — it books an automatic reversing entry dated today, so the ledger stays balanced and audit-clean.
Control accounts
The accounts the system posts to automatically: trade A/R and A/P (per currency), customer down-payments, supplier deposits, retained earnings, FX gain/loss, rounding. Configured once per company under Setup › Control Accounts.
Subledgers
An account flagged customer or supplier is a subledger — its balance is broken down by party. That is how the aging and statement reports work.

Admin

First-time setup

Do this once per company, roughly in this order. Most of it lives under Setup.

  1. Sign in and pick the company Log in with your email and password. If you have access to more than one branch, the company switcher appears at the top of the sidebar — choose the one you're working in.
  2. Currencies & the base currency Under Currencies, confirm the currencies you transact in exist (IDR, USD, EUR, AUD, MYR, HKD). The company's base currency is set on the company record (Setup › Companies) and cannot be changed once transactions exist.
  3. Chart of accounts Either import your existing chart (Chart of Accounts › Import from spreadsheet) or copy one from another company. See Chart of accounts below.
  4. Control accounts Setup › Control Accounts — map trade A/R, trade A/P, customer down-payment and supplier deposit accounts for each currency, plus retained earnings, rounding and realized FX gain/loss. The system refuses to post an invoice in a currency with no control account.
  5. Tax defaults Setup › Settings › Tax — PPN rate and input/output PPN accounts; PPh 23 rate and its payable / prepaid accounts. These pre-fill on new invoices.
  6. Opening balances Enter them as a single manual journal with source opening, balancing to Retained Earnings / Opening-Balance Equity. There is no separate opening-balance screen.
  7. Fiscal periods Setup › Periods — create the year's periods. Lock a period once it's closed so nothing back-dates into it; you can reopen if you must.
  8. Users, roles & branch access Setup › Users / Roles. Roles are permission sets (see Roles & permissions). On a user you can also restrict which companies (branches) they may switch into — leave it empty for "all branches".
Master data

Chart of accounts

Accounts have a code, a name, a type, and an optional parent (header) account. Codes are per-company, so branches can reuse the same numbering.

HTI's live chart uses 5–6 digit MYOB-style codes with a two-level header hierarchy — for example 42000 Sales B2B FIT – Full Tours under the 40000 Income header, 52000 COS B2B FIT under 50000, and the 61xxx/62xxx/63xxx operating-expense blocks. Per-currency control accounts sit at 12100 / 12110 / 12120 (trade A/R) and 21100 / 21120 / 21130 (trade A/P).

Building or importing it

  • Import wizard — Chart of Accounts › Import from spreadsheet. Map columns for code, name, type, parent code, currency. It reuses account-name aliases so re-importing updates in place.
  • Copy from another company — clone an entire chart to a new company from the account list.
  • Header accounts are groups (is_group) — you never post to them; leaves post, headers subtotal.
  • Cash flow section — each account carries an operating / investing / financing tag that drives the Cash Flow statement. Edit it on the account form.
Deleting accounts

An account with journal lines or child accounts cannot be deleted — deactivate it instead (the "Danger zone" on the account edit form).

Master data

Customers & suppliers

Both share one screen pattern. A party has a code, name, contact fields and NPWP. Customers also carry a client group (e.g. RTN, Scandinavia, Other TO) and country — these drive the Sales Overview report's grouping.

  • Auto-creation — imports and the API create missing parties automatically, with codes C#### / S####.
  • Custom fields — attach extra fields per party under Setup › Custom Fields (entity customer / supplier). Flag one "show in list" to get a filterable column on the party list.
  • Statement — open a party to see its running statement for a date range.
  • List search matches code, name, contact, NPWP and any custom-field value; results are paged.
Master data

Currencies & exchange rates

Exchange rates are per company, quoted against that company's base currency. Add them under Currencies — the screen reframes to "rates for {company}". A journal takes one currency and one rate; if you leave the rate blank the most recent rate on or before the entry date is used.

FX gain / loss

Settling a foreign-currency invoice at a different rate books realized FX to the gain/loss control accounts automatically. Unrealized revaluation is a manual journal for now.

Transactions

Purchases & supplier payments

Entering a purchase invoice

  1. Header — supplier, supplier's invoice/PO ref, invoice date, due date, currency + rate, description.
  2. Lines — pick an account (type to search the combo), optional Job, description, amount, service date. The Budget column is a quoted / expected cost baseline; it is left blank on manually typed lines and filled automatically on imported lines (see below).
  3. Tax — PPN % and PPh 23 %; use calc to compute from the line subtotal, or type the amounts.
  4. Save as a draft, then Post to book the journal (Dr expense/COS lines, Dr PPN input, Cr trade A/P, Cr PPh payable).

Editing a posted invoice is allowed — the app un-posts, re-saves and re-posts in one step, as long as no payment has been applied and the period is open. Void books a reversing entry.

Budget vs actual on purchase lines

A line's budget_amount is the planned cost; amount is what's currently booked; cost_source is manual, budget or actual. Imported and Jambix lines land as budget with budget_amount = amount. The Invoice Review pipeline flips a line to actual when the real supplier invoice arrives, freezing the budget for variance reporting.

Supplier payments & deposits

  • Purchases › Payments — record a payment from a bank account and allocate it across open invoices. The payment date must be on or after every invoice it pays.
  • Deposits — money paid to a supplier before an invoice exists posts to the supplier-deposit control account; apply it to an invoice later.
  • Payment List report — a flat "what to pay" list filtered by the invoice's promise-date custom field, laid out to mirror the BCA payment template.
Transactions

Sales & customer receipts

Sales invoices mirror purchases: header (customer, ref, dates, currency), lines (revenue account, Job, amount, and travel fields — pax, duration, party/traveller name), PPN/PPh, then Post (Dr trade A/R, Dr PPh prepaid, Cr revenue, Cr PPN output).

  • Sales › Receipts — record money in and allocate across invoices.
  • Customer deposits — down-payments received before invoicing sit in the customer-down-payment control account; apply them to invoices as they're raised.
  • List — filter by customer, search no./ref/description, and pick which columns (including custom fields) show.
Transactions

Banking

Banking shows every cash / bank account's balance and recent movements. An account is a "cash" account when it's flagged is_cash in the chart.

Transfer
Move money between two cash accounts, with an optional bank fee. Dr the destination, Cr the source (+ fee), Dr the fee account.
Spend
Quick multi-line payment: Dr the expense lines, Cr the bank. Source cash_payment.
Receive
Quick multi-line deposit: Dr the bank, Cr the income lines. Source cash_receipt.
Bank Book
Statement-style report for one account: opening, every movement with a running balance, money-in / money-out KPIs, closing.

Bank statement reconciliation

Banking › Reconcile — upload a bank statement (xlsx/csv), map columns, auto-match statement lines to unmatched ledger lines by amount and near date, add missing entries (fees, interest) straight from the workspace, then finish once the difference is zero. Reconciled statements can be reopened.

Transactions

Manual journals

Use Journals › New for anything the modules don't cover — accruals, reclasses, opening balances, adjustments. A journal has one currency + rate; lines carry account, optional Job, optional party (for subledger lines), memo, debit / credit. It must balance in the transaction currency before it will save.

  • Source tags the journal's origin: general, opening, adjustment, cash_payment, etc. Reports can filter on it.
  • Void posts a dated reversing entry rather than deleting; Ledger counts posted and void so the books stay balanced.
  • Search on the journal list matches the journal no./description/reference and line memos, account codes/names and party names.
  • Import — bring a batch of journals in from a spreadsheet; rows sharing a "group" column become one journal.
Month-end accruals

The Finance report's "extra Cost of Sales" is a monthly accrual journal — Dr COS 52000 / Cr A/P 21100 — for supplier costs known but not yet invoiced. Book it in the closing month and reverse it when the real invoice lands (or let Invoice Review actualise the budgeted line).

Transactions

Jobs (tour dossiers)

A Job is one tour dossier. Tag purchase and sales invoice lines to a Job and the app builds its P&L. Jobs carry a code (the Jambix DOSS NR), customer, arrival dates, pax, category, and Jambix reference figures (sales_ref / buy_ref — the quoted sell and buy).

The Job P&L page

Click a job code to open it. Top: revenue / direct cost / gross profit / net / margin KPIs for a date range. Then a table, one row per customer / supplier:

Pipelines

Invoice review — the supplier-cost check queue

Supplier invoices are dropped into a watched folder. An n8n workflow (or the standalone PowerShell watcher in n8n/) reads each file, extracts the line items with Gemini vision, and POSTs them to POST /api/v1/purchase/lines/review. Nothing hits the ledger yet — the batch lands in a queue for a human to confirm.

Working the queue

Transactions › Invoice Review (or Setup, depending on your menu). Each item shows the supplier's invoice line, the matched budget line, the variance and an over-budget flag.

  • Matching tries, in order: exact booking ref → the supplier's reservation code (res code) → supplier + service date within a window, narrowed by traveller name then budget amount. It never guesses between two candidates.
  • Supplier names are matched tolerantly — exact, then either-direction substring, then punctuation-stripped — so "BLACK HORSE BALI" finds "Black Horse".
  • Recheck re-runs the match (after you fix supplier names or add a booking ref). Confirm applies the actual cost: the line's amount becomes the real figure, cost_source flips to actual, the budget stays frozen, and the invoice re-posts.
  • Promise date set here writes through to the purchase invoice's Promise Date custom field, which the Payment List report reads.
  • Delete batch removes the queue rows only — it does not touch anything already posted.
Discount lines

Where the extractor produces a charge and its discount as two lines, the pipeline nets them by reservation code before submitting, so a single budget line is matched against the net cost.

Pipelines

Spreadsheet imports

Every importer follows the same four steps. Files are stored under writable/uploads/imports and re-parsed at each step, so you can go back and remap.

  1. Upload — choose an .xlsx, .xls or .csv file and the sheet.
  2. Map columns — tell the wizard which spreadsheet column is which field. Required fields are marked; some wizards also map account labels to your chart (aliases are remembered).
  3. Preview — a per-row table with a summary (ok / new / error counts) and every problem spelled out.
  4. Commit — creates the records. Most wizards can also post immediately, and offer a batch page with "post all drafts" / "revert drafts".

Pipelines

Budgets

A budget version ("Realistic 2026", "Optimistic 2026") holds one budgeted amount per P&L account per month, for one company and year. One version per year can be the default — that's what the P&L vs Budget report uses unless you pick another.

  1. Create a version — Setup › Budgets › New. Name, year, optional "default".
  2. Import your budget sheet — open the version, Import spreadsheet, map the account column and the twelve month columns. Header/non-P&L rows are skipped with a note.
  3. Adjust individual accounts — the version's grid has an Edit link per account for a twelve-month row form. Re-importing replaces figures for the accounts in the sheet, so tweaks survive a partial re-import.
  4. Report — Reports › P&L vs Budget. Pick a period and a version. Columns: Actual · Budget · Variance · Achieved %, grouped like the income statement. Every line that has an actual or a budget shows.
Reading the numbers

"Achieved %" is Actual ÷ Budget. For expense lines, over 100% means overspent. The GROSS / OPERATING / NET profit lines only show a % when the budgeted profit is positive — a "—" there means the budget is only partly loaded (e.g. opex entered but not revenue).

Reports built to replace spreadsheets

  • Sales Overview — the app version of the "Overview-YTD" sheet: per customer (or client group / country), revenue and pax for the window vs the same window a year earlier, with averages. Needs client group / country filled on the customer records to group.
  • P&L vs Budget — the app version of the Operational report's actual-vs-budget matrix and the Finance report's budget columns. Needs a budget version loaded.
  • Consolidated — straight sum by account code across companies sharing a base currency; no inter-company eliminations yet.
Where the numbers can drift from your Excel

The app values everything at the posted invoice rate and only counts what's actually in the ledger. Your workbooks use set FX rates and sometimes include budget or draft rows. Expect differences until all history is posted in the app.

Reporting

Month-end checklist

  1. Post everything — no lingering draft purchase / sales invoices, journals, receipts or payments for the month.
  2. Run Invoice Review to empty — actualise the budgeted supplier lines you have real invoices for.
  3. Accruals — book the "extra COS" and any other accrual journals for costs known but not invoiced; note which reverse next month.
  4. FX — enter month-end exchange rates; book any revaluation journal for open foreign-currency balances.
  5. Bank reconciliation — reconcile every bank account to its statement; difference must be zero.
  6. Aged AR / AP — review AR Aging and AP Aging; chase or write off as needed.
  7. Review the statements — Trial Balance foots, P&L and Balance Sheet look right, Cash Flow reconciles opening → closing cash.
  8. P&L vs Budget — check the month against the current budget version; write the commentary.
  9. Lock the period — Setup › Periods, so nothing back-dates in.
  10. Back up — run the DB backup script (see below).

Admin

REST API

A token-authenticated JSON API, built for Jambix and the n8n pipelines to push data in. Each token is bound to one company, so a request authenticates and scopes in a single step — no user session.

  • Tokens — Setup › API Tokens. Choose abilities (read, sales:write, purchase:write). The raw token is shown once; only its hash is stored.
  • Auth — send Authorization: Bearer <token> (or X-Api-Key).
  • Endpoints (all under /api/v1): GET ping, lookups (accounts, customers, suppliers, jobs), POST {sales|purchase}/invoices (idempotent on an external id), GET {sales|purchase}/invoices/{id}, POST jobs (reference figures only — no ledger effect), GET purchase/lines/pending, POST purchase/lines/costs, POST purchase/lines/review.
  • Full request/response detail: docs/API.md and the styled docs/api.html; a Postman collection is in postman/ (set your own token in the collection variable).

For the Malaysia (MYR) company, to submit sales invoices to LHDN's MyInvois system. Phase 1 only today.

Backups & updates

Backup

From the project root:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\backup-db.ps1

It reads .env, writes a timestamped, zipped mysqldump to backups/ and keeps the newest 20. That folder is git-ignored — copy the zips somewhere safe. On another machine the zip imports through phpMyAdmin.

Update loop

  1. Local: edit → commit → push.
  2. Server: git pull then php spark migrate.

Full first-time deploy (shared hosting or VPS), the production .env, CSRF and backup notes are in DEPLOY.md.

Before a non-local deploy

Set app.baseURL to the real host, turn CSRF protection on in the production .env, and run php spark key:generate so the encryption key is set (the e-invoice secret depends on it).

Roles & permissions

Every screen and action is gated by a permission. Permissions are bundled into roles under Setup › Roles, and each user is assigned one or more roles. The app ships with six starter roles — edit them, or build your own, on the Roles screen (the matrix there shows every role against every permission).

Starter roles

AdministratorEverything: post / void, close periods, master data, all reports, the dashboard, settings, users and roles.
AccountantPost & void journals, manage master data, close periods, open every report and the dashboard. No settings, users or roles.
ManagerRead-only: the dashboard and every report. Cannot create, post or change anything.
Auditor / ConsultantRead-only: every report. No dashboard; cannot change anything.
Staff / BookkeeperEnter and post journals. No dashboard or reports; cannot void or close periods.

Operational list and detail screens (Sales, Purchases, Journals, Jobs, master data) stay visible to any signed-in user — a "view-only" role is simply one with no editing permissions, so the New / Post / Edit buttons never appear for it.

Report access

The Report Center is gated in two layers: reports.view opens the hub, then one reports.<category> permission per category (Financial, General Ledger, Cash & Bank, Sales, Purchase, Job) decides which cards a role sees and can open. A role needs reports.view plus at least one category permission to see anything; a card the user can't open is hidden from the hub. To give one report a permission different from its category, list it in Config\Reports::$perms.

Branch (company) access

Separate from roles: on a user's Setup › Users record you can limit which companies they may switch into. Leave the branch list empty for "all active companies"; add rows to restrict them.

Glossary

Base currency
The company's functional currency. Reports are always in it.
Control account
An account the system posts to automatically (A/R, A/P, deposits, FX, retained earnings, rounding).
Subledger
A control account whose balance is broken out by customer or supplier.
Dossier / Job
One tour booking. Its code is the Jambix DOSS NR.
Jambix
The tour-operations system. It pushes bookings and quoted figures to this app via the REST API; the ledger is not affected by those pushes.
GOP
Gross Operating Profit — revenue minus cost of sales, before operating expenses.
EBITDA
Here, revenue minus COS minus operating expenses (depreciation, interest, tax and FX all sit below it).
Budget / actual / manual
A purchase line's cost_source. budget = a quoted / expected cost; actual = confirmed from the real supplier invoice; manual = typed by hand with no separate budget.
Promise date
The date a supplier invoice is expected to be paid — a custom field on the purchase invoice, driven by Invoice Review, read by the Payment List report.
Res code
The supplier's own reservation reference. A match key in Invoice Review.
PPN
Indonesian VAT. Input PPN on purchases, output PPN on sales.
PPh 23
Indonesian withholding tax — deducted from a supplier payment (payable) or withheld from you by a customer (prepaid).
External id
A caller-supplied key that makes an imported / API-created invoice idempotent — re-sending the same key returns the existing record.
Pinned announcement
A notice that shows as a dismissible banner on every page; dismissal is remembered per browser.
